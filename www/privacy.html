Privacy Policy

Word Pop: Definition · Last updated November 16, 2026

Word Pop: Definition is published by [YOUR NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information.

We do not collect personal information

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about players.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score for each level on your own device so it can show it to you later. This never leaves your device, and it is erased when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Read-aloud

Words and definitions are read aloud by the text-to-speech voice already built into your phone or browser. The game does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

No network connections

Everything the game needs is built into the app, including its fonts and all 1,200 words. The game does not contact any server while you play.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Word Pop: Definition is a vocabulary game intended for adults. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
